why i made this

i wanted a way to show what i'm reading, and actually talk about it

it started at half price books.

the whole idea, on a shelf

the stands at half price books

i kept noticing the little display stands in bookstores and libraries. a few books propped up face-out: a pick, something new, something worth a look. when they want to show different books, they swap them out one by one.

i remember thinking: what if you could change the whole thing all at once?

then i saw people doing it at home

the same idea was on people's own bookshelves. you can find those black display holders on etsy and amazon, with a book propped up facing out like a tiny gallery piece. people clearly wanted their shelves to say something about what they love.

so i made one that changes in ten seconds

marginalia is a small e-paper frame that shows the cover of the book you're reading right now. start something new, update it from your phone, done. no app, no account.

what i like most is that it gives you a reason to talk about the book you're in the middle of. someone glances at your shelf and asks, "oh, how is that one?" it's also just a nice thing to have on a shelf.
